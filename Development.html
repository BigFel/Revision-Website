Different Types of Development

Hope this helps you understand the different approaches to software development.

Traditional (Waterfall) Development

Description: Linear process where each phase must be finished before the next begins.

Advantage: High predictability and clear documentation.

Disadvantage: Very difficult and expensive to change requirements later.

Outsourcing Development

Description: Contracting development work to external companies or individuals.

Advantage: Access to specialized skills and cost savings.

Disadvantage: Potential communication issues and loss of control over the process.

Customisation Development

Description: Tailoring software to meet specific client needs.

Advantage: Highly tailored to client requirements and user experience.

Disadvantage: Can be time consuming and expensive to develop and maintain.

Agile Methods Development

Description: Iterative and incremental development approach that emphasizes collaboration and flexibility.

Advantage: Faster delivery, better adaptability to changes, and improved team collaboration.

Disadvantage: Requires strong team commitment and can be difficult to manage without proper training.
